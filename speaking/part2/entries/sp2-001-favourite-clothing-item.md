# Describe a Favourite Item of Clothing

## Cue card

Describe an item of clothing you particularly like to wear.

You should say:
- what it is
- when you got it
- how often you wear it

and explain why you like it so much.

## Model answer (Band 8-8.5, ~1.5-2 min spoken)

I'll talk about an old leather jacket that's honestly seen better days, but I can't quite bring myself to get rid of it.

I picked it up secondhand at a market stall about four years ago — completely on impulse, actually, because I'd gone there looking for something else entirely. It's a slightly battered brown leather jacket, worn in at the elbows in that way that only happens over years of use, which I suspect is exactly why I liked it in the first place; it didn't feel like something fresh off a rack.

I probably wear it two or three times a week, mostly in autumn and early spring when it's too cold for just a shirt but not cold enough to justify a proper winter coat. It's become a bit of a default, honestly — the kind of thing you reach for without really thinking about it because it goes with almost everything else you own.

What I like about it goes a bit beyond how it looks, though. There's something reassuring about an item that's already lived a life before you owned it — you're not the one putting the first scuff on it, so you don't feel precious about it, which oddly enough makes it more comfortable to wear than something brand new. And practically speaking, leather like that only gets better with age, unlike most fabrics, which start falling apart the moment you actually use them.

So yes — not glamorous, not new, but it's the one thing in my wardrobe I'd genuinely be annoyed to lose.
