# Interview Rescheduling — Formal Letter to Admissions Tutor

## Prompt

You have an interview arranged with a college admissions tutor, but you have a conflict on that day.

Write a letter to the tutor. In your letter:
- explain the situation and apologise for the conflict
- suggest an alternative time for the interview
- ask about the length of the interview and whether it will include a test

Write at least 150 words. Begin: *Dear Sir or Madam,*

## Model answer (Band 8-9, ~190 words)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing regarding my interview for the BSc Computer Science programme, currently scheduled for Thursday 6th August at 10:00 am. I am very sorry to say that I am no longer able to attend at that time, as I have a hospital appointment for a minor procedure booked for the same morning, which cannot be moved without a further six-week wait for a new slot.

I would therefore like to ask whether it might be possible to reschedule my interview to later that same day, perhaps at 3:00 pm, or alternatively to the following morning, Friday 7th August. Either option would allow me to attend without further disruption to your schedule.

I would also be grateful for a little more detail about the format of the interview. Could you tell me roughly how long it is expected to last, and whether it will include a written or technical test alongside the discussion? If a test is involved, I would appreciate knowing broadly which subject areas it covers, so that I can prepare appropriately beforehand.

Thank you very much for your understanding, and I apologise again for any inconvenience caused.

Yours faithfully,
Gabriel Rossi

## Examiner notes (why this clears 8-9, not 7-7.5)

- **Named institution + course**: "BSc Computer Science programme" — anchors the letter instead of a generic "my interview."
- **Causal justification with a consequence**: not "I have another appointment" but a specific, non-negotiable reason *and* the cost of not accommodating it ("a further six-week wait").
- **Narrow, concrete rescheduling window**: same-day or next-morning, not "sometime next week" — this is the single most common gap between 7.5 and 8+ in GT Task 1.
- **Layered follow-up**: not just "how long / is there a test" but a third clause deeper — "if so, which subject areas," matching genuine Band 9 question-stacking.
- **Register**: "Dear Sir or Madam" paired correctly with "Yours faithfully" (name unknown); formal throughout, no contractions.
