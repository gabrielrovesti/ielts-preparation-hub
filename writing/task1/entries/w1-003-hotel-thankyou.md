# Thank-You Letter to Hotel Manager

## Prompt

You recently stayed at a hotel and want to thank the manager for the excellent service you received during your stay.

Write a letter to the manager. In your letter:
- say when you stayed at the hotel and why
- describe what impressed you most about the service
- explain how this made a difference to your stay

Write at least 150 words. Begin: *Dear Sir or Madam,*

## Model answer (Band 8-9, ~170 words)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my sincere thanks for the excellent service I received during my stay at your hotel from 14th to 17th July, when I was in the city attending a family wedding.

What impressed me most was the attentiveness of your front-desk team, in particular a member of staff named Elena, who went out of her way to arrange a late check-out on my final day without my even having to ask, once she noticed my flight had been delayed. Housekeeping was equally impressive: the room was immaculate on arrival, and a small oversight I mentioned in passing — a missing extra pillow — was resolved within minutes.

These small but genuine touches made a considerable difference to what was otherwise quite a stressful few days of travel and family logistics. I left feeling that the stay had been looked after by people who actually cared about getting the details right, rather than simply following a checklist.

Please pass on my thanks to your whole team.

Yours faithfully,
Gabriel Rossi

## Examiner notes

- Named individual staff member + concrete, small-scale detail (missing pillow, late check-out) rather than generic praise ("everyone was so nice") — this is what separates Band 8 from Band 6-7 in this letter type.
- "How this made a difference" bullet answered causally (busy family trip → the service reduced stress), not just restated as a compliment.
- Correct formal closing given unnamed recipient.
